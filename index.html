About

VladisLive Dev is an independent horror game developer focused on creating slow, oppressive experiences built on atmosphere rather than shock. The goal is simple: make the player feel watched, uncomfortable, and unsure — without relying on cheap tricks.

Atmosphere First

Dark spaces, limited information, subtle audio, and visual restraint are the core of every project.

Slow Pressure

Fear grows gradually. Tension matters more than speed or constant interaction.

Grounded Horror

Stories take place in familiar environments where something feels slightly wrong.
